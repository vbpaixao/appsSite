How It Works

Redact a Photo in 3 Steps

No manual tracing, no upload sites, no “trust us”: the whole job happens on your phone.

1

Pick a photo & auto-detect

Open your photo and flip on the Auto-Anonymizer. On-device AI finds faces, text, or both: tune the sensitivity and preview what it caught, or select regions manually with zoom.

2

Choose your mask

Blur for subtlety, pixelation for the broadcast look, black box for real secrets, emoji for fun: in circular or rectangular areas, per face or per region.

3

Scrub & export

Strip EXIF and GPS metadata in the same pass, then save as PNG, JPG, HEIC, or a secure PDF: resized to fit wherever it's going.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about blurring, censoring, and anonymizing photos on iPhone.

How do I blur a face in a photo on iPhone?

Open the photo in Redact Photo and turn on the Auto-Anonymizer, on-device AI detects every face and applies your chosen mask in one tap. Manual selection with zoom is there for anything else. Learn More →

Can it blur multiple faces automatically?

Yes. The AI detects all faces in a group photo at once, raise the sensitivity to catch more, preview the regions, and mask everyone in a single pass. Learn More →

How do I hide text or personal info in a screenshot?

Switch detection to Text (or Faces & Text) and the app finds text regions automatically, names, numbers, addresses. Mask them and export a clean copy. Learn More →

Is blurring enough to hide sensitive text?

Not always, researchers have shown light blur and coarse pixelation on text can sometimes be reversed. For real secrets use Black Box: fully opaque, flattened into the export, nothing to recover. Learn More →

How do I hide my child's face before posting a photo?

Auto-detect faces, then pick the mask that fits the moment: a soft blur, a circular mask, or an emoji. Share the memory, keep the face off the public internet. Learn More →

Can I put an emoji over a face?

Yes, choose the Emoji style and every detected face gets the emoji you pick. Works in the combined Faces & Text mode too. Learn More →

How do I blur a license plate in a photo?

Draw a rectangle over the plate with manual selection (zoom in for precision) and apply blur, pixelation, or a black box: perfect for marketplace and car-sale listings. Learn More →

Can I export a redacted photo as a PDF?

Yes, after redacting, choose Save as PDF. Ideal for censored documents and paperwork; JPG, PNG, and HEIC with resizing are also available. Learn More →

Does Redact Photo remove GPS and EXIF metadata too?

Yes. The EXIF panel shows what the file carries, GPS, camera info, timestamps, and you can strip any or all of it from the export. Learn More →

Is Redact Photo free?

Free to download, with a fully unlocked 3-day free trial on subscriptions. Continue weekly or annually, or buy lifetime access once: every plan unlocks everything. Learn More →
