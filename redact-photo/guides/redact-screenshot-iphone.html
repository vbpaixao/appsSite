How to Redact a Screenshot on iPhone

Redact Photo Guides · Updated July 2026 · 3 min read

Quick answer: Open the screenshot in Redact Photo, set Detection Type to Text, and the AI finds every text region: balances, usernames, emails, order numbers. Black-box the private parts and export a copy that's safe to share.

Sharing a screenshot is sharing a slice of your accounts: the support thread wants your order screen, the group chat wants the funny exchange, the forum wants your settings, and all of them arrive with your real name, email, balance, or address in a corner you stopped noticing months ago.

Screenshots Leak More Than You Notice

The private data in a screenshot is usually furniture: the account email in the top bar, the notification that slid in mid-capture, the last four digits that are actually enough, the username that links to everything else you've posted. You're blind to it because you see it every day. Text detection isn't, it flags every readable region, including the ones you'd have scrolled past.

Redact a Screenshot (Step by Step)

  1. Open the screenshot in Redact PhotoStraight from your screenshots, nothing is uploaded, which matters when the content is your bank app.
  2. Run Text detectionEvery text region lights up. Check the preview against what you actually want visible, the point of the screenshot stays, the identity leaves.
  3. Black-box the private regionsFor account data, use Black Box rather than blur: fully opaque, flattened into the image, nothing to enhance. Manual selection with zoom handles anything detection didn't box tightly.
  4. Export and share the redacted copySave as PNG or JPG and share the clean version. The original screenshot stays in your library.

Make It Actually Unrecoverable

Blurred text in screenshots is the textbook case for recovery attacks, clean fonts on flat backgrounds are exactly what deblurring tools are best at. That's why the recommendation here is the black box, not the aesthetic choice; the evidence is laid out in can blurred text be recovered? Redact Photo flattens masks into the export, so there's no “blur layer” for anyone to peel off.
