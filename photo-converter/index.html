FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about converting, resizing, and cleaning up iPhone photos.

How do I convert HEIC to JPG on iPhone?

Select the photos in the Photos app, tap Share, choose “Convert with PhotoConverter,” pick JPEG, and save. The whole batch converts on-device in seconds — no website, no emailing yourself. Learn More →

Why are my iPhone photos HEIC?

Since iOS 11, iPhones shoot HEIC by default: same quality as JPEG at roughly half the size. The trade-off is that Windows, Android, and many web forms can't open it. Learn More →

Why won't HEIC photos open on Windows?

Windows needs paid codec extensions to display HEIC, so iPhone photos often arrive unopenable. The reliable fix: convert to JPG on the iPhone before sending. Learn More →

Can I convert photos without uploading them to a website?

Yes. Photo Converter processes everything 100% on-device — your photos are never uploaded to a server, unlike free web converters. Learn More →

Can I batch convert many photos at once?

Yes. Select dozens of photos in Photos and convert them all in one tap — same format, size, and quality settings applied to the whole batch. Learn More →

How do I turn photos into a single PDF on iPhone?

Select the photos — receipts, IDs, paperwork — tap Share → “Convert with PhotoConverter,” and choose PDF as the output format. Learn More →

How do I compress a photo to fit an upload limit?

Resize by dimensions or target a file size directly — watch the live output size (2.1 MB → 0.23 MB) until it fits the form, email, or Discord limit. Learn More →

How do I remove GPS and EXIF data from iPhone photos?

Enable “Remove all metadata” during conversion. EXIF, GPS, camera details, and timestamps are stripped in one tap — before the photo ever leaves your phone. Learn More →

Does converting reduce photo quality?

Only if you ask it to. Keep original dimensions and maximum quality for visually lossless output, or deliberately trade quality for a smaller file. Learn More →
