How to Merge Voice Memos on iPhone

SmartMerge Guides · Updated July 2026 · 3 min read

Quick answer: The built-in Voice Memos app has no merge feature. The fastest way to merge voice memos on iPhone: select your recordings in Voice Memos → tap Share → choose “Merge with SmartMerge” → drag them into order → tap Merge. You get one seamless file with chapter markers in under a minute.

You recorded an interview in three parts, or a lecture that got split when a call came in, and now you need it as one file. If you've been hunting through the Voice Memos app for a “combine” button, you can stop: it isn't there.

This guide shows you the fastest way to merge voice memos on iPhone, entirely on your phone, with no computer, no GarageBand project, and no uploading private recordings to a website.

Why You Can't Merge Recordings in the Voice Memos App

Apple's Voice Memos app records, trims, and replaces sections of a single memo, but it has never offered a way to join two recordings together. The workarounds people usually find are painful: building a multi-track GarageBand project for a simple join, transferring files to a computer, or uploading personal audio to a free web tool of unknown ownership.

A dedicated voice memo joiner solves this with one trick the others can't match: a native share extension. Because SmartMerge lives inside the iPhone share sheet, merging starts right where your recordings already are.

How to Merge Voice Memos with SmartMerge (Step by Step)

  1. Select your recordings in Voice MemosOpen Apple Voice Memos, tap Edit (or long-press a recording), and select every clip you want to join. You can pick as many as you need, SmartMerge handles hours of audio.
  2. Share to SmartMergeTap Share and choose “Merge with SmartMerge” from the share sheet. Your clips open in SmartMerge instantly, no exporting, no importing, no file juggling.
  3. Order your clips and set Auto-TrimDrag and drop the clips into the order you want. Optionally turn on Auto-Trim to cut 2, 3, or 5 seconds of mic fumbles and dead air from the start and end of every clip.
  4. Merge, then share or saveTap Merge. Seconds later you have one seamless audio file with a chapter marker where each clip begins, ready to share, save to Files, or archive.

What You Get After the Merge

The output is a single audio file, the same format and quality as your originals, with zero quality loss. SmartMerge also adds a chapter marker at the start of every original clip, so a merged three-part interview still behaves like three navigable parts inside one file.

Tips for a Clean Merged Recording

  • Use Auto-Trim. The first seconds of most memos are fumbling and taps. Auto-Trim cuts 2, 3, or 5 seconds from every clip's edges in one pass.
  • Check the order before merging. Voice Memos sorts newest-first; drag clips into chronological order in SmartMerge before you tap Merge.
  • Rename the result. Give the merged file a real name (“Thesis Interview, Full”) so it's findable in Files later.
